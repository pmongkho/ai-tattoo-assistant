User Data Deletion

Last updated: January 15, 2025

We respect your right to control your personal information. This page explains how account owners and clients can request deletion of data processed by AI Tattoo Assistant.

1. Delete Your Account

  1. Sign in to your AI Tattoo Assistant dashboard.
  2. Open Settings → Account and select Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the prompts. We will schedule deletion of your account, automations, and associated content within 30 days unless legal obligations require retention.

2. Request Deletion for a Client

If one of your clients requests removal of their data, please email us using the template below so we can locate the relevant records.

Email Template

Subject: Client Data Deletion Request
Body: Include your studio name, the client's name or handle, approximate date of interaction, and any identifiers (phone, email, social profile link) that help us locate the conversation.

3. Where to Send Requests

Send all deletion requests to privacy@aitattooassistant.com. We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete eligible deletions within 30 days.

4. Data We Cannot Immediately Delete

Some information may be retained temporarily to comply with law, prevent fraud, or resolve disputes. When retention is required, we will inform you of the basis and the expected deletion timeline.

5. Need Help?

Contact our privacy team at privacy@aitattooassistant.com or call +1 (555) 010-2025 if you have questions about our data deletion process.
